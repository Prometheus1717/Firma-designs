What is Astrocartography?

Astrocartography (also called Astro*Carto*Graphy or locational astrology) is a branch of astrology that maps your natal chart onto the Earth's surface. Developed by astrologer Jim Lewis in the 1970s, it creates a personalized world map showing lines where each planet aligns with the four major angles of your chart:

  • Midheaven (MC) — where a planet culminates overhead. Associated with career, public reputation, and ambition.
  • Nadir (IC) — where a planet is at its lowest point. Associated with home, roots, emotional foundation, and family.
  • Ascendant (ASC) — where a planet is rising on the eastern horizon. Associated with identity, first impressions, and personal energy.
  • Descendant (DC) — where a planet is setting on the western horizon. Associated with partnerships, relationships, and collaboration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is astrocartography?

Astrocartography (also called locational astrology) maps your birth chart onto the Earth's surface. It shows lines where each planet was rising, setting, culminating, or at its lowest point at the moment you were born. These lines indicate places in the world where specific planetary energies are strongest for you — influencing career, relationships, creativity, and personal growth.

Natal Navigator uses the astronomy-engine library for high-precision planetary position calculations.
