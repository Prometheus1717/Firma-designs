How these cities were chosen

Most zodiac city lists online are vibes with a headline. Frequently asked questions

Can astrology really tell me where to live?

It can tell you something useful: which environments feed the qualities your chart emphasises. A sun-sign guide does this at the broadest level; astrocartography does it precisely, by mapping the planetary lines of your exact birth moment onto real geography. Neither is a command — both are reflection tools for a decision only you can make.

What's the difference between zodiac city lists and astrocartography?

A zodiac list describes one-twelfth of people at once, based only on the sun sign. Astrocartography is calculated from your full birth data — date, exact time and birthplace — and produces lines and city ratings unique to you. The list is a starting point; the map is personal.

Which zodiac sign moves the most?

Mutable signs — Sagittarius and Gemini above all — relocate most readily; Sagittarius chases horizons and Gemini chases stimulation. Fixed signs (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius) move rarest but commit hardest when they do.
