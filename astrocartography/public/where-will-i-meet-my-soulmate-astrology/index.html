The short answer

Most soulmate astrology answers when or who. Astrocartography answers the question you actually typed: where. Your birth chart, projected onto a world map, draws specific lines — above all your Venus lines and your Descendant (DSC) lines — along which meeting, attraction and partnership are traditionally read as coming easier. Those lines cross real countries and real cities. That's your where.

Key takeaway: Astrology's answer to “where will I meet them” is your Venus and Descendant lines — and you don’t have to move there. A trip taken along your Venus geography counts.

The two lines that answer 'where'

Your Venus lines. Venus rules attraction, affection and being enjoyed. Along a Venus line — especially Venus rising (ASC) or Venus setting (DSC) — you tend to feel more open and more attractive, social life warms, and romantic chemistry appears with less effort. It's the classic 'find love here' geography.

Your Descendant (DSC) lines. The Descendant is the partnership angle of your chart — the doorway through which significant others arrive. Any planet's DSC line colours the kind of partner and meeting: Venus DSC is the storybook version; Sun DSC brings partners who see you; Jupiter DSC favours meeting someone while travelling or learning. People repeatedly report that their defining relationships trace back to places near their DSC lines — which is exactly what the technique predicts.

How to actually use this

Step one: get your exact birth time (certificate, not memory) — the Descendant moves fast, and 'roughly 9am' can shift your love lines by hundreds of kilometres. Step two: generate your astrocartography map and locate your Venus ASC, Venus DSC and other DSC lines. Step three: look at the cities inside those lines' bands — that's your shortlist of soulmate geography.

Then be practical about it. You don't have to move to Buenos Aires because Venus grazes it — people use these lines for choosing between job offers, picking the city for a sabbatical or even choosing holiday destinations with intention. Love found on holiday on your Venus DSC line counts just as much.

What this can and cannot promise

Astrocartography describes conditions, not appointments. A Venus DSC line makes a city read like fertile ground — it does not schedule the meeting, and staying home on your sofa neutralises any line. Treat the map as permission and direction: it tells you where openness comes easier for you, and openness is the part of love you can actually control.

And if your current city sits on none of your love lines? That is information too — not a verdict. Many people simply plan more travel along their Venus geography and let the odds do the rest.

Frequently asked questions

Which line shows where I'll meet my partner?

The Descendant (DSC) lines — the partnership angle of your chart — and above all the Venus DSC line, which combines the partnership doorway with the planet of love. Venus ASC is the strong second: it raises your own openness and attractiveness wherever it runs.

Do I have to move to meet someone on my love lines?

No. Lines work on any timescale — a semester, a sabbatical, even a two-week trip taken with intention. Many people use their Venus and DSC geography to pick travel destinations rather than a new home.

How exact does my birth time need to be?

As exact as possible — the angles that define your DSC lines shift quickly. Use your birth certificate; a 20-minute error can move a line by hundreds of kilometres.

What if I meet someone away from all my lines?

Then you met someone — the map describes tendencies, not exclusivity. Astrocartography highlights where connection tends to come easier for you; it never claims love is impossible elsewhere.
