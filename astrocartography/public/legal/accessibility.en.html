Accessibility Statement

Natal Navigator iOS app · Version of 14 August 2026

We want Natal Navigator to be usable by as many people as possible, and we treat accessibility as ongoing work, guided by WCAG 2.1 level AA. This page describes honestly what the app supports today, where its limits are, and how to reach us when something is in your way.

What the app supports

  • Light and dark appearance, following your system setting or your manual choice.
  • Reduce Motion is respected throughout: loading animations, the thinking indicator, the globe's idle rotation and decorative movement stop when your system asks for less motion.
  • Interface controls carry accessibility markup (roles, labels, states); purely decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technologies.
  • Everything the visual chart wheel and the globe show is also available as plain text: placement lists, aspect lists, house tables and the searchable city list.
  • Dialogs can be closed with the Escape gesture, and focus returns to where you were.
  • Generous tap targets and a clear, consistent tab structure.

Known limitations

  • Operating the interactive chart wheel and the 3D globe with a screen reader is limited. The same information is available in the text lists named above, which we treat as the accessible path.
  • The app's text size does not yet follow the system text size setting (Dynamic Type). This is on our list.
  • Full VoiceOver coverage is still being tested on hardware; individual screens may be inconsistent.

Feedback and contact

If anything blocks you, please tell us: info@natalnavigator.com. Accessibility reports are handled with priority, and your report directly shapes what we fix next.

Status of this statement

Prepared on 14 August 2026 based on an internal review of the app (version 1.0). We update this page as the app changes.
