Privacy Policy

Natal Navigator iOS app · Version of 14 August 2026

The short version: Your birth data and your chart live on your device, not on our servers. There are no accounts. The only data that leaves your device is what you actively send when you use the Guide chat or the translation feature, plus anonymous purchase information handled by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not run ads and we do not track you across other apps.

2. Data that stays on your device

When you set up the app you enter a display name, your date, time and place of birth, and preferences such as language and theme. All of this is stored only locally on your iPhone (app storage). Your birth chart, the world map lines, city lists, readings and daily texts are calculated on the device. We have no server copy of your profile and cannot see it. Deleting the app, or using "Reset all data" in the You tab, permanently removes this local data (server-side fair-use counters are covered in section 3).

Birthplace search. While you type your birth city, the app first searches a city catalogue that ships inside the app. From two characters on, your search text is also sent to Apple Maps (MKLocalSearch) on the device to find places the catalogue does not contain. This request goes to Apple under Apple's own privacy terms; we do not receive or store it. The time zone is derived from the chosen coordinates on your device.

App start. Opening the app requests only the current remote configuration from our server, without any identifier. We record no launch or activity data.

3. The Guide chat and translations (AI features)

The Guide chat and the translation of reading texts are the only features that send content off your device — and they do so only after you have explicitly allowed it in the AI consent dialog shown before the first transfer. You can review and revoke this permission at any time under You → AI data sharing; after revoking, nothing is sent until you allow it again. When these features run, the following is transmitted to our API server (hosted on Vercel) and processed there:

  • your question and, for context, up to the last six messages of the current chat,
  • calculated facts from your chart that are needed to answer (for example planet positions, line distances, house placements), which can include your birth date, time and place,
  • for translations: the English texts to be translated,
  • a random per-install identifier (used for the daily question allowance) and your IP address (used for hourly abuse limits).

AI processing. To generate answers and translations we send this content to external AI model providers acting as our processors. Primary provider is DeepSeek (Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd., China); as fallback we may use Z.ai (GLM models) or xAI (USA). These providers process the content to produce the response. Where providers are located outside the EU/EEA (China, USA), the level of data protection may differ from EU standards; we transmit only what is needed for your specific request, and you decide whether to use these features at all. The rest of the app works without them.

Transfers outside the EU/EEA. For providers in countries without an EU adequacy decision (in particular China), the transfer is based on your explicit consent given in the AI consent dialog after being informed of the possible risks (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). Where a US provider is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, we rely on that certification; otherwise the transfer is likewise based on your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time under You → AI data sharing; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that happened before the withdrawal.

What we store server-side: only counters (number of requests per install identifier per calendar day, per IP address per hour, and total cost) to enforce fair-use limits. We do not store your questions, answers or chart facts on our servers. The counters contain no content. "Reset all data" clears your device, gives you a fresh install identifier and thereby detaches the old counters from you; on request (info@natalnavigator.com) we delete the counter rows for your install identifier from the database.

Storage periods. Hourly counters that reference an IP address are deleted automatically after 7 days at the latest. Daily counters that reference the random install identifier are deleted automatically after 12 months at the latest. Aggregate counters without any personal reference are kept for statistics. Reported answers in the operations log expire automatically with the log's short retention window, at most 90 days.

If you report a Guide answer via "Report answer", the reported question and answer are transmitted and kept in our operations log so we can review them; nothing else is attached.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for sending content to the AI providers; performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for answering your request; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for abuse prevention.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold through Apple's App Store and charged to your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. To manage entitlements, trials and renewals we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) as a processor. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase/receipt information from Apple, and provides us aggregated statistics (for example trial conversions). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. RevenueCat participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

5. What we do NOT do

  • No accounts, no e-mail collection in the app, no address books, no location tracking. The app never requests your device location; the birthplace search is described in section 2.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, no sale of data. The app therefore does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt: there is nothing to ask for.

6. Hosting

Our API runs on Vercel Inc. (USA/EU edge network); requests are subject to Vercel's standard technical logging. The fair-use counters are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted in the EU (Supabase). Data Processing Agreements are in place with our processors.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because your profile exists only on your device, access and erasure are literally in your hands: the You tab shows everything, and "Reset all data" or deleting the app erases it. For anything concerning the server-side counters, contact us at the address above. We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects concerning you.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children and provides no content intended for them. We do not knowingly process children's data.

9. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act gives you specific rights. In the last 12 months we have collected only these categories of personal information: identifiers (a random per-install identifier; your IP address for hourly abuse limits), user content (Guide questions with chart context, only when you use the Guide after consenting), and commercial information (purchase status, processed by Apple and RevenueCat). We collect them for app functionality only, as described in sections 3 and 4, and keep them for the periods stated there. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we have not done either in the past 12 months; there is therefore nothing to opt out of. We do not knowingly collect information of consumers under 16. You have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information, the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights, and the right to use an authorized agent. To exercise any right, e-mail info@natalnavigator.com; because we hold no account data, we verify requests via your install identifier, and we will walk you through locating it in the app if needed. We respond within the statutory deadlines.

10. Changes

We will update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available in the app (You tab) and at natalnavigator.com.
